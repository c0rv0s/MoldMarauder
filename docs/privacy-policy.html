Privacy Policy for Mold Marauder

Last updated: April 2, 2026

Mold Marauder respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may access, how that information is used, and your choices.

Information the app may access

  • Gameplay and progress data. The app stores gameplay information such as your progress, unlocked molds, currency, settings, and similar save data so the game can function properly.
  • iCloud data. If iCloud is available on your device and account, the app may use Apple's iCloud services to sync certain game progress or settings across your devices.
  • In-App Purchase data. If you make purchases, Apple processes the transaction. The app may receive product and transaction status information needed to deliver purchased content.
  • Camera access. If you use the app's augmented reality features, the app may request camera access so AR content can be displayed.

How information is used

  • To save and restore your gameplay progress.
  • To sync supported progress through Apple's iCloud services.
  • To offer, process, restore, and validate in-app purchases through Apple.
  • To provide AR gameplay features that require the device camera.

Information sharing

The app does not sell your personal information.

The app relies on Apple services for features such as iCloud, App Store transactions, and device permissions. Information handled through those services is subject to Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Mold Marauder is not intended to use third-party advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs for cross-app tracking.

If that changes in a future release, this Privacy Policy should be updated before release.

Data retention

Game save data may remain on your device, and any synced data may remain in your iCloud account, until it is deleted by you or removed through normal app or account management.

Your choices

  • You can deny camera access in your device settings, although AR features may not work correctly.
  • You can manage iCloud features through your Apple account and device settings.
  • You can manage in-app purchases and subscriptions through your Apple account settings, where applicable.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe personal information has been provided in error, contact support.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
